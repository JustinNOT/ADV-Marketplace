FAQ

Are decals removable?

Yes—premium paint-safe vinyl. No residue when removed correctly.

What areas do you cover?

We operate where there is demand. Tell us your target neighbourhoods and we’ll match suitable drivers.

How do you verify drivers?

We confirm car photos, city, approximate weekly mileage, and route suitability for campaign goals.

Who prints the decals?

Local vinyl print partners. Printing and delivery are included in campaign quotes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes—month-to-month campaigns. Remove decals and you’re done.

Do clients get reporting?

Yes—basic mileage and coverage summaries for each active campaign.
