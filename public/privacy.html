Overview

We collect driver and listing information to operate the marketplace. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Data We Collect

  • Contact details (name, email, phone)
  • Location (city, province/state, country, postal code)
  • Vehicle details (make, model, year, color, seats)
  • Usage details (weekly mileage, routes, availability)
  • Photos you upload
  • Optional social links
  • Server logs (IP, user-agent)

How We Use Data

  • Display approved listings to potential clients
  • Moderation, abuse prevention, and security
  • Communications about your listing

Legal Basis

Your consent and our legitimate interests in operating the marketplace.

Sharing

We don’t sell personal data. Photos and listing details are public once approved. We may share minimal data with hosting or analytics providers to run the service.

Retention

Listings persist until you request removal or we remove them under our policies. Backups and audit logs may persist for a reasonable period.

Your Choices

  • Request access, correction, or deletion: privacy@advehicles.example
  • Opt out of marketing at any time.

Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and moderation. Avoid uploading sensitive personal data.

International Transfers

Data may be processed where our providers operate. We take steps to protect it appropriately.

Changes

We may update this policy; check the last updated date.
